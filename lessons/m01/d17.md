# M1-D17 — AEC product hero copy

## Today's summary

You write a B2B problem headline for a non-configurator product idea.

## What this is & why you need it

Founders who can't state the problem don't get meetings. This is Track-2 muscle before Track-2 starts.

## How the session works (90 min)

Pick a pain from ShapeDiver patterns (no client secrets) · write hero · CTA · friend test

## Tool steps (do these in order)

1. HTML section.
2. Plain language.
3. Ask to critique fluff — you rewrite.

## The concept

Formula: who · painful job · outcome. Avoid feature lists. Not a configurator — respect your ShapeDiver lane.

## Annotated example

```
<section>
  <h2>Stop re-typing model issues into email threads</h2>
  <p>For BIM coordinators who need a single list of clashes with owners and due dates.</p>
  <a href="#">Join the waitlist</a>
</section>
```

## Where beginners go wrong

1. Feature soup.
2. Configurator idea.
3. Jargon nobody outside your firm uses.
